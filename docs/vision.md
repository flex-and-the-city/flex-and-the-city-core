# Vision: Flex and the City

## 1. Executive Summary
**Flex and the City** is a mobile workout tracker designed for the adaptable urban athlete. Unlike rigid tracking apps, it prioritizes flexibility and speed, allowing users to maintain progress consistency across different gym environments, equipment variations, and weight units.

---

## 2. The Problem Statement
Standard workout applications often fail in real-world urban scenarios where:
* **Equipment varies:** A user’s "Home Gym" has different machines than their "Office Gym."
* **Units conflict:** Switching between gyms that use Kilograms (kg) and Pounds (lbs) creates "mental math" friction and logging errors.
* **Availability is limited:** In crowded city gyms, a planned machine may be occupied, forcing the user to choose an alternative that the app doesn't recognize as "equivalent."

## 3. Core Purpose
The purpose of this app is to bridge the gap between a **structured plan** and **unpredictable reality**. It exists to provide all relevant history and progress data in a way that allows the user to follow their plan smoothly, regardless of which gym they are in or what equipment is available.

## 4. Primary User
**The Urban Nomad Lifter:** A gym-goer who values consistency but trains in multiple locations. They need a tool that is fast, works offline, and handles the "small adjustments" of gym life without breaking their data history.

---

## 5. Strategic Pillars
To remain true to the vision, the project focuses on three key principles:
1. **Frictionless Logging:** The "Open-to-Log" flow must be under three taps.
2. **Exercise Flexibility:** Support for "Variations" (e.g., swapping a Barbell for Dumbbells) while maintaining a single progress line for that movement.
3. **Unit Fluidity:** Seamless, on-the-fly conversion between KG and LBS at the set level.

## 6. Minimal User Flow (The "Golden Path")
1. **Open:** See the scheduled plan for the day immediately.
2. **Log:** Enter reps/weight for the first exercise.
3. **Adapt:** If a machine is busy, swap to a pre-defined variation.
4. **Complete:** Finish the session with an automatic save and PR (Personal Record) update.

---

## 7. Success Criteria (Definition of Value)
The project is successful if a user can complete a full week of training across two or three different gyms—adjusting for missing equipment and varying weight units—without feeling that the app hindered their workout flow.
